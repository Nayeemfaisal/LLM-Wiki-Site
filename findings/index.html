Separate August source screen · 22 August 2026

Samsung broadband EIS record added as a controlled candidate

  • What is published: four Samsung ICR18650-26J cells with repeated EIS measurements from 100% to 10% SOC and raw voltage/current acquisition files.
  • What it can test: a small, traceable join between acquisition data and EIS at known SOC states.

Upcoming week · 23–30 July 2026

DRT competitor review

  • GP-DRT: an open-source Gaussian-process method for recovering a distribution of relaxation times from EIS data.
  • Useful feature: it reports a DRT estimate together with covariance, so uncertainty can be recorded instead of hiding it.
  • Stress cases: the project documents tests involving noise, overlapping processes, truncated frequency ranges, inductive features, and experimental EIS.
  • IEST guide: explains the inverse-problem difficulty and the practical balance between regularisation, resolution, and noise suppression.
  • Important limit: these references analyse EIS and DRT. They do not by themselves validate our time-series-to-EIS dataset matching.
  • Our research gap: connect matched time-series cycling data, EIS, and DRT-ready outputs with traceable cell, SOC, temperature, and protocol metadata.
  • Next experiment: run GP-DRT on selected Panasonic EIS files, save the DRT mean and uncertainty, test reconstruction at held-out frequencies, and compare with a simpler baseline.
  • Decision: use GP-DRT as a technical comparator and uncertainty reference, not as proof that the full TS + EIS + DRT bridge works.

Dataset intake · 29 July 2026

Three candidates added to the research queue

  • Samsung ICR18650-26J paired records: a current/voltage record and an EIS record are published as companion data for the same SOC-estimation study. The required check is whether cell IDs, SOC points, and cycle records can be joined file by file.
  • WO3 Li-ion electrode record: Zenodo publishes raw charge-discharge and EIS CSV files. It is useful for a small importer and DRT-format check, but it is a material-level half-cell study, not the primary full-cell validation dataset.
  • Cycle + EIS + CV material record: a Mendeley package states that it includes cycle, EIS, CV, and full battery data. It remains a screening candidate until the files show repeatable IDs and compatible impedance columns.
  • Decision: this week we will inspect the metadata and a small file sample from each source before downloading large archives or calling any candidate a match.

New dataset screen · 23 July 2026

Five external datasets compared

  • Best full match: Cambridge uneven-usage EIS, with same-cell GCPL and GEIS records in the local manifest probe.
  • Strong candidates: NASA PCoE and CALCE, both with same-cell time-domain or capacity evidence plus impedance evidence.
  • Partial candidate: Battery Archive, because the public page shows cycling data but does not confirm an EIS match.
  • EIS control: the 54-cell Zenodo set has EIS and machine-readable metadata, but no time-series side.
  • Next: use a frozen cell-level holdout, reconstruction error, residual checks, and uncertainty before calling any source validation.
