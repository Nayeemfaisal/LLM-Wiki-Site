LFP COS and EIS workbooks: structural link found, scientific join not yet proved

This audit inspected the raw source files rather than relying on repository folder names. It confirms which fields exist and records what is still missing.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

COS structure

82,052 time-series rows

Raw date, time, voltage, current, cycle, capacity, energy, internal resistance, and dV/dt fields are present.

EIS structure

Frequency and impedance fields

The ACIM sheet contains device/test/channel/cycle/step identifiers, frequency, impedance magnitude, and phase.

Reproducible

Files checksummed

Both inspected workbooks have recorded paths, sizes, sheet dimensions, and SHA-256 values.

Boundary

Join not supported

Different channels and report times mean the shared condition label cannot establish same cell or same state.

What the raw files show

Both modalities are visible, but their relationship must be demonstrated

  • COS workbook: an 82,052-row measurement table with time, voltage, current, capacity, cycle, and internal-resistance fields.
  • EIS workbook: an 8,319-row time-series table and a 22-row impedance table containing frequency, magnitude, phase, and test-context fields.
  • Shared label: both artifacts are explicitly associated with the 0.05 A charge condition.
  • Critical difference: the reports use different channels and separate report-start times, so the current evidence does not prove a shared battery state.
Research decision

Use for structural tests now; hold the scientific match claim

  • Allowed now: parser development, time-series structural checks, and documented EIS format handling.
  • Not allowed yet: training a COS-to-EIS model, interpreting DRT against a time-series state, or claiming a matched validation pair.
  • Next audit: locate cell, SOC, temperature, and repeat mapping in the source documentation or raw metadata.
  • Stop rule: if no mapping exists, retain the result as a clear structural benchmark and do not force a pairing.
