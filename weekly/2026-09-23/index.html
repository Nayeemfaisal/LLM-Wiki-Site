A supervisor-provided review was assessed and translated into practical requirements for the TS + EIS + DRT pipeline.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

Paper assessed

From production to prediction

  • Source: Hassini, Redondo-Iglesias, and Venet, Lithium-Ion Battery Data: From Production to Prediction (2023).
  • Main message: battery datasets are heterogeneous because the experiment, instrument, sampling, file format, and use case differ.
  • Pipeline implication: raw values alone are not sufficient; each imported source needs provenance, measurement context, units, and identifiers.
  • Why it fits this project: our proposed TS-to-EIS join needs a documented cell, state, temperature, and protocol relationship before DRT is interpreted.

DRT meaning

Concrete update to our workflow

Four gates before model work

  • Gate 1: source integrity. Keep a read-only record of what was downloaded and from where.
  • Gate 2: experiment context. Identify cell, chemistry, test stage, SOC, temperature, protocol, and units.
  • Gate 3: TS-to-EIS join. Prove the shared state key or report that the source cannot support the match.
  • Gate 4: DRT quality. Run EIS and reconstruction checks before interpreting relaxation features.
