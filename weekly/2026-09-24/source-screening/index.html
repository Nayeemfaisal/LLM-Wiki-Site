Candidate 1

NASA PCoE lithium-ion battery ageing data

  • Reported: charge, discharge, and impedance operations at multiple temperatures during ageing.
  • Potential value: the experiment design may support a TS plus impedance audit with temperature context.
  • Current limitation: the public landing page does not provide an immediately attached data resource; legacy-archive access and terms remain to be checked.
  • Decision: hold until one lawful archive sample proves stable battery, cycle, operation, and impedance identifiers.

Candidate 2

Broadband EIS with voltage and current acquisition across SOC

  • Reported: raw voltage/current acquisition files and broadband EIS at different states of charge.
  • Potential value: a tightly scoped SOC-aware source is appropriate for a first file-level matching test.
  • Required check: prove the cell, SOC, repeat, and test-condition relationship between an acquisition record and an impedance spectrum.
  • Decision: priority one for the next manifest-backed pass, hold, or reject decision.

Method benchmark

RWTH EIS Data Analytics

  • Reported: Lin-KK, equivalent-circuit, and DRT fitting workflows with EIS-based state-estimation examples.
  • Potential value: a documented method comparator for structural EIS checks and later DRT analysis.
  • Current limitation: it does not establish a new TS-to-EIS dataset relationship for this project.
  • Decision: use after reviewing example-data provenance and licence, and after a primary source has passed file-level auditing.

Audit standard

Every new source must earn its place

Before source data enter an adapter, the manifest must include provenance, licence, cell or sample ID, test context, state, temperature, protocol, units, file hash, and the explicit time-series-to-EIS join rule. Missing evidence is a research result, not a detail to hide.
