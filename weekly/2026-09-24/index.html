Source 1

LG MJ1 ageing, charging, and BEV-profile data

  • Published coverage: ten LG Chem INR18650 MJ1 cells with capacity/resistance ageing data, BEV profiles, charging curves, and quasi-stationary OCV.
  • Pipeline value: strong for time-series loading, ageing-state metadata, partial-charge analysis, and OCV-related features.
  • Boundary: EIS is not established by the public description, so it is not presented as a TS + EIS + DRT match.

Source 2

196-cell experimental degradation study

  • Published coverage: 196 commercial NCA/silicon-doped graphite cells under calendar, cycling, and dynamic conditions.
  • Repeated labels: capacity, resistance, loss of lithium inventory, loss of active anode material, and loss of active cathode material.
  • Pipeline value: a large TS-to-SOH and degradation-label benchmark for adapter design and robust ageing analysis.
  • Boundary: it is not a direct DRT validation dataset unless raw impedance data are explicitly found and joinable.

TUM dataset · Associated study

Research decision

How these sources connect to TS + EIS + DRT

  • Direct TS + EIS + DRT sources: remain the LFP EIS/pulse, fast-charging ageing, degradation-path, online-EIS, and A123 candidates.
  • TUM sources: strengthen the time-series, ageing, SOH, OCV, and degradation-label side of the research framework.
  • Cross-source use: a future comparison can test whether TS-derived ageing indicators transfer to EIS/DRT sources, but no transfer claim is made before controlled evaluation.
  • Immediate next step: build one small source manifest and decide pass, hold, or reject based on actual fields and identifiers.

Source screening · 24 September 2026

Three resources added with defined roles and evidence limits

  • NASA PCoE: repeated charge, discharge, and impedance operations make it a valuable legacy-archive screening candidate. The archive layout and stable join keys remain unverified.
  • Broadband SOC EIS: reported raw voltage/current acquisition and broadband impedance at different states of charge make it the most focused new potential state-aware link.
  • RWTH EIS Analytics: a reproducibility benchmark for Lin-KK, circuit fitting, and DRT methods. It is not treated as a new scientific matching dataset.
  • Next decision: build one small manifest for the broadband source and record a pass, hold, or reject result from the actual files.
