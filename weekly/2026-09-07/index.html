New battery data: one clear candidate, three controlled follow-ups

This update records what was verified from public source descriptions, what remains unknown, and the backend work added before any DRT analysis begins.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

Verified candidate

A123 LFP multimodal electrochemical dataset

  • Published coverage: 71 A123-type LFP cells with charge-discharge, EIS, CV, ICA, and PITT records.
  • Time-series side: each charge-discharge workbook contains voltage, current, capacity, and time-series data.
  • EIS side: one EIS text file is published for each numbered cell.
  • Why it is promising: matching cell numbers provide a concrete first join key.
  • What is not claimed: matching filenames alone do not prove the same SOC, temperature, test stage, or measurement protocol.

EIS quality reference

Oxford multisine EIS

Raw current and voltage signals are published for multisine EIS. The most immediate use is checking signal quality and stationarity assumptions, not claiming an ageing-data match.

Backend work completed

Structural EIS intake endpoint added

  • Endpoint: POST /intake/eis in the local API/HPC demo.
  • Checks: expected columns, finite numeric values, positive frequency, duplicate frequency count, and frequency ordering.
  • Output: a transparent JSON report with a clear drt_input_ready flag.
  • Limit: it is only structural screening. Kramers-Kronig, reconstruction, residual, stability, and uncertainty checks still belong to the scientific DRT workflow.
