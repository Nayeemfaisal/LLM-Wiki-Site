Research method8 September 2026

Dataset source audit: proving a match before modelling

This protocol turns public-source screening into a small, reproducible experiment. It records evidence first and treats a matching claim as the result, not an assumption.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

Core finding

A shared cell ID is necessary, but it is not sufficient

  • Needed: a cell or sample identifier that appears in the TS and EIS material.
  • Also needed: compatible SOC, temperature, cycle or ageing state, protocol, timestamp or test-point information, and units.
  • Result: no source is labelled “matched” until these fields are verified from downloaded files and metadata.
Candidate-specific audit plan

First broad audit

A123 LFP multimodal

  • Published structure links charge-discharge and EIS files by cell number.
  • Audit one cell's spreadsheet and EIS text file.
  • Confirm whether state, temperature, and test order support more than a cell-level join.

What was added to the repository

  • Audit template: a CSV with identifiers, state fields, units, licence, hash, and evidence location.
  • Read-only inventory tool: records local paths, extensions, sizes, SHA-256 checksums, and simple CSV/TXT/TSV headers.
  • DRT evaluation protocol: separates file screening from EIS quality, impedance reconstruction, stability, and uncertainty checks.
