Dataset priority review: choose the next small experiment

Eight public sources now have defined roles. The ranking is deliberately simple: it helps choose what to audit next, while the raw source files still decide whether a match is real.

Research workspace maintained by Nayeem Faisal.

How ranking works

Score evidence, not claims

  • Highest weight: public evidence of a direct TS-to-EIS relationship.
  • State context: SOC, temperature, cycle, or test condition increases audit value.
  • Ageing context: supports the degradation research question, but does not replace a state-aware join.
  • Access, licence, and schema: reduce practical risk when a small sample is audited.
  • Important: this is a planning score only. It is not validation, model accuracy, or a DRT result.

Controlled experiment

What happens before model training

  • Download the smallest lawful source sample.
  • Run the read-only inventory and store file hashes plus headers.
  • Complete a manifest with state, units, protocol, and evidence links.
  • Make a pass, hold, or reject decision on the TS-to-EIS join.
  • Only then run structural EIS intake, quality checks, DRT reconstruction, stability, and uncertainty review.
